Appetizers

Nuts, Cheese & Ham Plate

A classic European-style charcuterie featuring premium cured ham, artisanal cheese selection, and roasted mixed nuts. The perfect start to your evening, offering rich, savory flavors that pair beautifully with any of our cocktails—ideal for sharing and setting the tone for a convivial gathering.

Main Course

Margherita Pizza

A timeless Italian classic with fresh mozzarella, vibrant tomato sauce, fragrant basil, and a perfectly crispy crust. Simple yet sublime, this pizza celebrates quality ingredients and traditional preparation—comfort food that never goes out of style and pairs wonderfully with our citrus and herbal cocktails.

Potato Salad

Creamy, comforting potato salad with tender potatoes, crisp vegetables, and a delicate dressing that brings everything together. A beloved side dish that adds heartiness to your meal—its mild, satisfying flavor makes it the perfect companion to both our lighter and bolder menu offerings.

Saikyo-yaki Fish (西京焼き)

Delicate fish marinated in sweet miso paste and grilled to perfection, offering a beautiful balance of umami, sweetness, and tender flaky texture. This traditional Japanese dish showcases refined flavors and expert technique—an elegant choice that pairs exceptionally well with our sake-based cocktails and tea creations.

Dessert

Matcha Cheesecake

A luxurious fusion dessert combining the earthy sophistication of premium matcha with rich, creamy cheesecake. The subtle bitterness of green tea beautifully balances the sweet creaminess—the perfect ending to your meal that pairs wonderfully with our coffee cocktails or as a standalone indulgence to savor slowly.
